#Task 5 – Compare Rule-Based and AI-Based chatbots

| Feature       | Rule-Based Chatbot             | AI-Based Chatbot                           |
| ------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Technology    | Predefined rules               | Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning |
| Learning      | No learning                    | Learns from data                           |
| Responses     | Limited                        | Flexible                                   |
| Understanding | Follows exact/programmed rules | Understands different forms of questions   |
| Complexity    | Easy to build                  | More complex                               |
| Suitable for  | FAQs                           | Smart assistants                           |
| Example       | Simple FAQ bot                 | AI conversational assistant                |
